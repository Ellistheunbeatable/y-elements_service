Y-Elements is the youth service of Westend City Churches — a Saturday gathering for young people who want to worship, encounter the Word, and build faith together.

Bring your joy, your questions, your energy, and the friend who has been meaning to come. Here, worship is vibrant, the message meets real life, and fellowship is part of the story.

Whether you already know the songs or this is your very first time, you are specially invited. Come ready to lift your voice, listen closely, and leave with something good to carry into the week.

05 / Good to know

First time?
Start here.

Everything you need to feel ready for your Saturday. The main thing to remember: you are specially invited, and you do not have to come alone.

See the service details ↗ Open directions ↗
Who is Y-Elements for?
Y-Elements is the youth service of Westend City Churches. If you are young, vibrant, and hungry for God, this is your home.
What happens during the service?
Expect an atmosphere of vibrant worship, the Word, and true youth fellowship — the heart of the Y-Elements gathering.
When do you meet?
This and every Saturday at 3:00 PM sharp. Set a reminder, gather your friends, and make the time for it.
Where is The Life Cathedral?
The Life Cathedral is in Ofaakor, Kasoa. Open directions in Google Maps before you set out.
Can I bring a friend?
Absolutely. The invitation is to come with a friend, come with energy, and make Jesus famous together.
What if it is my first time?
You are specially invited. Come as you are, bring an open heart, and join in at your own pace. We would love for you to be part of the gathering.
